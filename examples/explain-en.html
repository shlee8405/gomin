Checkout is slow because tax is computed three times

Each cart change triggers a full tax recalculation in the API, the pricing service, and the client. Computing it once on the server and caching per cart cuts p95 from 2.8s to about 0.6s.

In short
  • 2.1s of the 2.8s p95 is spent in calculateTax(), called 3× per request.
  • The tax result only depends on cart contents and address, so it is safe to cache.
  • Recommended fix: compute once in the API and pass it down. About one day of work.
